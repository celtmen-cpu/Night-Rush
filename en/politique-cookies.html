Cookie policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026

1. What is a cookie / tracker?

A cookie is a small file placed on your device when you visit a website. A tracker allows collecting navigation information. Some require your prior consent, in accordance with article 82 of the « Informatique et Libertés » law and CNIL recommendations.

2. Trackers used on this site

  • Google Analytics (cookies _ga, _ga_*) — audience measurement, retention: maximum 13 months. Loaded only after your consent.
  • Umami — audience measurement without cookie (aggregated and anonymous data). Loaded only after your consent.
  • Local storage (localStorage) — used only to remember your consent choice. This storage contains no navigation data and remains on your device.

No tracker is loaded until you have made your choice in the consent banner.
