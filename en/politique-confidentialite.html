Privacy policy

Last updated: 30 August 2026 — GDPR compliant (Regulation (EU) 2016/679)

1. Data controller

The data processing described below is carried out by Clément (pseudonym « Celtmen »), publisher of the Night Rush website, as data controller within the meaning of article 4 of the GDPR. Contact: celtmen@yahoo.com.

2. Data collected and purposes

a) Audience measurement

The Site uses Google Analytics and Umami to understand Site usage and improve its content. These tools are loaded only after your consent. Data concerned: pages visited, visit duration, device type, anonymized IP address.

b) Download statistics

When a visitor clicks a download link, a message is sent to a Discord server (webhook) indicating the chosen platform (Windows, Mac, iPhone, Android). The visitor's IP address may transit in Discord's technical logs. These data help us know the number of downloads per platform (legitimate interest).

c) Tournament ranking

The public ranking (Tournament page) displays the pseudonym and best score of players, stored on the Supabase database. The pseudo and score are created in the game and displayed in accordance with tournament rules (legitimate interest).

d) Satisfaction survey

The survey form is provided by Google Forms. Only the information you choose to enter is collected. This service is a third-party processing: we invite you to consult its own policy.

3. Legal bases for processing

  • Consent (art. 6.1.a GDPR): deposit of audience measurement trackers (see the cookie policy).
  • Legitimate interest (art. 6.1.f GDPR): download statistics and display of tournament ranking.

4. Recipients and subcontractors

Data may be transmitted to the following subcontractors, who act only on our instructions:

  • Google LLC (Google Analytics, Google Forms) — USA;
  • Umami (cloud.umami.is) — audience measurement without cookie;
  • Discord Inc. (download webhook) — USA/EU;
  • Supabase Inc. (tournament ranking) — USA/EU;
  • GitHub Inc. (Site hosting).

Transfers to the USA are governed by appropriate safeguards (standard contractual clauses, adequacy decision where applicable).

5. Retention periods

  • Audience measurement trackers: maximum 13 months (CNIL recommendation);
  • Tournament ranking: duration of the tournament then archiving (1 year);
  • Technical logs (webhook): in accordance with Discord's policies.

6. Your rights

In accordance with articles 15 to 22 of the GDPR, you have rights of access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability and objection over your data, as well as the right to withdraw your consent at any time.

To exercise these rights, contact us at celtmen@yahoo.com. We commit to responding within one month. You may also file a complaint with the CNIL (cnil.fr).

7. Security

The Site is distributed exclusively over HTTPS. Access to services (Supabase, webhook) is protected by restricted API keys that do not allow access to data outside the perimeter described above.

8. Modifications

This policy may be updated at any time, notably to follow legal or technical developments. The applicable version is the one published on this page with its update date.
